Filled a new glamping site to 100% four months ahead and gained 10,000 followers on a $500 marketing budget

100% occupancy $500 budget 10,000 followers Rating 4.58

Launched a new glamping site (HORECA): on a $500 budget I filled it to 100% four months ahead and gained 10,000 followers in the first month.

Project context

During regular analysis of growing markets I spotted an opportunity in the region's tourism industry. Developing transport infrastructure, road construction and a rising tourist flow created favorable conditions for launching accommodation.

A full hotel project required significant investment, so I decided to test the business model through a glamping format — with a lower entry barrier and high potential payback.

My scope covered every part of the project except the technical construction and installation of the units.

Objectives

  • Validate the investment attractiveness of the market.
  • Develop the concept of the future project.
  • Build a financial model and calculate unit economics.
  • Find a suitable location and agree on placement terms.
  • Launch an MVP on a minimal budget.
  • Quickly fill the site and test demand.
  • Collect guest feedback and define directions for further development.

What was done

Market research and business model

Analyzed the region's tourism market and found steady growth in demand for countryside getaways.

Built a financial model and calculated unit economics. Defined the payback range for a single glamping unit:

  • optimistic scenario — 8 months;
  • conservative scenario — 14 months.

For the technical part I brought in a specialized partner responsible for construction, installation and operation of the units.

Product creation

Developed the project concept, positioning, brand platform, visual identity, customer scenarios and the service structure with add-on services.

In parallel I negotiated with land owners and took part in signing the lease agreements.

Go-to-market and launch

To test the hypothesis I launched an MVP at minimal cost. The main focus was on:

  • social-media seeding;
  • work with bloggers;
  • organic content;
  • building a community around the project.

After launch I built separate sales funnels for two audience segments:

  • tourists and travelers;
  • locals — for events and celebrations.

Additionally I introduced lead management, a loyalty program and repeat sales.

Working with the product after launch

After the first months of operation I ran CustDev interviews with guests.

Based on the feedback I shaped a list of product improvements and defined points for further growth.

Once demand was confirmed, I started raising investment to scale the project.
