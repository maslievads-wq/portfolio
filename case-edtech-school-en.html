Market TOP-10 400,000+ followers 80M+ reach $80,000/mo

Built and scaled an online school from scratch: niche market TOP-10, 400,000+ followers and 80M+ reach over 3 years.

Project context

The client was an expert in tailoring and sewing with 20+ years of experience, an own fabric store and a premium atelier.

The goal was to build and scale an online school from scratch, turn expertise into an educational product and become one of the leading players in the niche online-education market.

The project ran over three years.

Objectives

  • Research the audience and identify the most in-demand educational products.
  • Test demand for different product hypotheses.
  • Build a scalable client-acquisition system.
  • Build a webinar sales funnel.
  • Organize collaboration between marketing, CRM and sales.
  • Scale the project to new regions.
  • Strengthen the school brand and the expert's recognition.
  • Become one of the leading online schools in the industry.

What was done

Product discovery and audience research

Ran CustDev interviews and analyzed the needs of the target audience.

Based on the data I shaped and tested 25+ product hypotheses, offers and marketing messages.

Through performance marketing I ran a series of tests and selected three most promising learning directions with confirmed market demand.

Building the sales system

Designed and implemented a full webinar sales funnel:

  • participant acquisition;
  • registration and audience segmentation;
  • communication automation;
  • attendance analytics;
  • CRM integration;
  • lead qualification;
  • handover to the sales team;
  • control of key conversion metrics.

Built the processes connecting marketing, sales and analytics.

Scaling marketing

Launched and scaled acquisition channels:

  • Meta Ads;
  • Google Ads;
  • content marketing;
  • social media;
  • email marketing;
  • retargeting.

The average monthly ad budget was around $80,000. After validating the model I scaled the project to additional regions and new product lines.

Brand development

Developed and ran a long-term content strategy to grow the expert's recognition and build audience trust.

Content marketing became one of the key drivers of organic growth and reduced dependence on paid traffic.
