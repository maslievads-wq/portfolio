$1.20 per install in Canada and $1.50 in the USA: a Google UAC test for a mobile app in Tier-1 countries

$1.20 CPI (Canada) $1.50 CPI (USA) 5,842 installs Google UAC

Google UAC test for a mobile app in Tier-1 (Canada and USA): 5,842 installs, $1.20 CPI in Canada and $1.50 in the USA — validating the funnel, retention and unit economics.

About the project

  • Client: NDA;
  • Niche: mobile app;
  • Channel: Google Ads / Universal App Campaigns (UAC);
  • Geography: Canada and USA;
  • Goal: validate the acquisition funnel, retention and unit economics;
  • Optimization model: app installs;
  • Test period: January — March 2024;
  • Ad budget: $7,950.35;
  • Result: 5,842 installs.

Objective

The client needed to test mobile-app promotion in Tier-1 countries and understand whether Google Ads can be used as a user-acquisition channel.

The main goal wasn't just to get installs, but to validate the whole first part of the funnel: impression → click → install → first use → retention → unit-economics assessment.

At the start it was important to understand:

  • how much an install costs in Canada and the USA;
  • whether there's enough traffic volume;
  • how users behave after install;
  • what retention the acquired users show;
  • whether the traffic can be scaled further;
  • whether the product economics add up at the current acquisition cost.

Approach

For the test we used Google Ads App Campaigns (UAC). The format suits mobile-app validation because Google distributes impressions across placements itself:

  • Google Search;
  • YouTube;
  • Google Display Network;
  • Google Discover;
  • Google Play;
  • partner app placements.

We split campaigns by country to evaluate install cost and user quality in Canada and the USA separately.

What we tested

Install cost in Tier-1 countries

We needed to understand at what price users can be acquired in expensive geos. Canada and the USA were analyzed separately.

First-funnel performance

We checked how well the ads bring the user to an install. We measured impressions, clicks, CTR, click-to-install conversion rate and cost per install.

Retention after install

The point wasn't just cheap installs, but whether users stay in the product after the first touch: first open, post-install activity, D1 / D7 / D30 retention, traffic quality by country.

Unit economics

After the first data we needed to see how the acquisition cost relates to a user's potential value: install cost, user quality, retention, post-install events, scaling potential and the path to a profitable model.

Key result

Canada delivered the best result — $1.20 per install. In the USA the cost was $1.50 per install. The average across the two countries was $1.36 CPI. For Tier-1 markets that's a strong result, especially at the test stage before full scaling.
