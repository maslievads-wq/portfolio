Took a B2B SaaS product to break-even within 3 months of the MVP launch

Break-even in 3 mo B2B SaaS Performance + CPA 6 market segments

Took a B2B SaaS (CRM for service centers) from MVP to break-even in 3 months — through market segmentation, performance and CPA channels.

Project context

A CRM system for service centers that automates key business processes: device intake and handover, order management, inventory, warranty service, customer base and financial control.

At onboarding the product was at the MVP stage and needed a client-acquisition system and validation of the business model.

Objectives

  • Find effective client-acquisition channels.
  • Build segmentation of the service-company market.
  • Develop value propositions for different audience segments.
  • Create a scalable sales system.
  • Test the economic efficiency of acquisition channels.
  • Take the product to break-even.

What was done

Market research and segmentation

Analyzed the market of service companies and identified the key client segments:

  • shoe-repair shops;
  • watch repair;
  • small service workshops;
  • home-appliance service centers;
  • service chains;
  • automotive service centers.

For each segment I built a separate map of needs, pains and usage scenarios. Based on the research I shaped unique value propositions and marketing messages.

Building the lead-generation system

As the main growth channels I chose Performance Marketing and Affiliate Marketing (CPA model).

For different segments I developed specialized lead magnets and acquisition materials. Launched test campaigns with subsequent optimization by conversions and lead quality.

Community and opinion leaders

To speed up market penetration I organized work with industry opinion leaders. In parallel I built an event-marketing system:

  • participation in industry events;
  • product presentations;
  • use-case demonstrations;
  • collecting feedback from prospects.

Building the sales funnel

For the long decision cycle I built a nurturing webinar funnel. Inside the system I implemented:

  • lead segmentation;
  • client qualification;
  • automated communication flows;
  • work with dormant leads;
  • re-engagement of the audience.

This increased conversion from interest into commercial negotiations and onboarding to the system.
