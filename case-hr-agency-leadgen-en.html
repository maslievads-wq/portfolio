356 leads at $2.53 for an HR agency

356 leads $2.53 per lead Meta Ads 4 regions

Lead-gen test for an HR agency on Meta Ads (Ukraine / Belarus / Kazakhstan / Georgia): 356 career-consultation leads at $2.53 via lead forms.

Objective

The HR agency needed to attract people who are looking for a job or want to change profession but don't know where to start: how to build a CV, where to find vacancies, how to pass interviews and how to improve their chances of getting hired.

The main goal of the campaign — a steady flow of leads for career consultations at a clear price. It was important to test demand across several regions at once:

  • Ukraine;
  • Belarus;
  • Kazakhstan;
  • Georgia.

What was promoted

We promoted a consultation for people who need help finding a job. The audience's main pains:

  • can't find a job for a long time;
  • the CV gets no responses;
  • doesn't know where to move in their career;
  • fear of interviews;
  • no clear search strategy;
  • wants to relocate or work remotely;
  • wants to change field but doesn't know how to position themselves.

Hypothesis

We assumed the audience responds better not to abstract 'HR consulting' but to a concrete promise: 'We'll help you figure out why you can't find a job and build a clear search plan.'

So the ads focused not on the agency, but on the person's problem: no responses, uncertainty about the CV, difficulty finding a job and the wish for a clear career strategy.

What was done

For the test I launched several ad directions:

  • Job search — the main hypothesis for people actively looking for a job who want results faster;
  • Career consultation — a broader angle for those unsure of their direction;
  • CV review — for the audience already searching but getting no responses;
  • Webinar / consultation — a softer entry for those not yet ready to submit a request;
  • Look-alike and interests — testing audiences based on similar users and career interests.

Every campaign was tested separately to see which combo delivers not just traffic, but real leads at the lowest cost.

Result

The best-performing campaign was 'HR Consulting | Job Search | Lead Gen | Ukraine / Belarus / Kazakhstan / Georgia' — the main working combo for further scaling:

356leads
$2.53per lead
$901.68ad spend

What the test showed

The key takeaway: there is demand for career consulting in the chosen regions, but the audience responds best to a direct pain — job search — rather than to a generic 'career consultation' offer.

Wordings like 'let's review your career' performed weaker. Concrete promises worked better:

  • help find a job;
  • review search mistakes;
  • improve the CV;
  • prepare for applications and interviews;
  • build a clear action plan.

Why the campaign worked

A clear pain

The ads spoke to people with a current problem: they're looking for a job and not getting the desired result.

Simple request mechanic

The user didn't need to go to a website and study the service — the request was collected via a lead form inside Meta.

The right geography

Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan and Georgia gave enough audience volume while keeping the lead cost low.

Fast cut-off of weak hypotheses

Underperforming campaigns weren't scaled — budget was reallocated toward the combo with the best lead cost.
