From Market Niche to International Brand: Glaze Glass Launch & 30M+ Reach

30M+ reach B2C & B2B models International expansion Market leader positioning

Developed and launched Glaze Glass as an international smartphone screen protector brand, building a recognized consumer brand in a commodity market. Executed across Europe and CIS markets with dual B2C e-commerce and B2B partnership models achieving 30M+ reach.

Project Context

The smartphone screen protector market was divided into two segments: cheap no-name products with unstable quality and expensive premium products with high markups. A quality mid-tier segment was underdeveloped.

The challenge was to find a free market position and create a product combining stable quality, affordable pricing, simple installation process, strong visual identity, recognizable branding, and B2C/B2B scalability.

Market Research

Conducted comprehensive market analysis and competitive intelligence:

  • Price segments and product characteristics
  • Customer reviews and expectations
  • Competitor positioning and assortments
  • Marketplace product listings and packaging
  • Competitor content performance and engagement formats
  • Online and offline sales specifics

Research confirmed a market gap for a quality product in the mid-price segment with strong branding and clear positioning.

Product Strategy

Product development focused on three key principles:

  • High quality without premium markup
  • Simple self-installation capability
  • Distinctive visual identity

Developed an applicator for easy installation and reduced error probability.

Branding & Positioning

Most category brands used technical or aggressive names. Named the brand Glaze Glass — evoking smoothness, shine, and beauty.

Brand identity became the foundation for all visual design:

  • Bright pink packaging
  • Soft, emotional style
  • Clear visual metaphor
  • Strong differentiation from dark, technical competitor packaging

Added gamification to the installation experience — a hidden donut image inside the applicator, reinforcing emotional brand connection.

Go-To-Market Strategy

Launched with two independent growth models:

  • B2C: Direct sales via e-commerce, marketplaces, content, and performance marketing
  • B2B: Wholesale sales to electronics shops, service centers, retail chains, and distributors

Developed separate landing pages for B2B with different offers and communication logic, emphasizing partner margins and repeat sales.

B2C Marketing

Focused on direct customer acquisition emphasizing:

  • Self-installation in seconds
  • High quality
  • Affordable pricing
  • Oleophobic and hydrophobic coating
  • Durability
  • Distinctive design

B2B Marketing

Used Meta Ads, Google Ads, Yandex Direct, specialized landing pages, and dedicated sales team for partner acquisition.

Content Strategy

Developed a scalable content system built around both features and emotions:

  • Installation demonstrations
  • Durability tests
  • Comparison with cheap alternatives
  • Coating demonstrations
  • Daily use scenarios
  • Product stories
  • UGC content
  • Entertainment videos
  • Lifestyle content
  • Educational posts
