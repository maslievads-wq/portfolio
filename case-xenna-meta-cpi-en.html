Xenna launch: $0.90 CPI on Tier 1 countries

$0.90 CPI Tier 1: US/UK/CA Meta Ads Mobile app installs

Test launch of the mobile game Xenna on Meta Ads across Tier 1 markets (US/UK/Canada): validated the first funnel stage and found a combo with $0.90 CPI.

Objective

Before scaling, I needed to check how well the acquisition funnel works on expensive Tier 1 markets. The goal of the test was not to reach maximum installs right away, but to validate the first funnel stage: creative → click → store visit → app install.

I needed to understand:

  • whether the audience responds to the game;
  • which creative approaches deliver cheaper installs;
  • the real CPI on Tier 1 countries;
  • whether Meta Ads can be the main primary-acquisition channel;
  • which combos to keep for further scaling.

Starting point

The project was at the test-launch stage. Before scaling it was important not to burn budget on broad campaigns, but to quickly validate different hypotheses.

The nuance — Tier 1 countries (US, UK, Canada) usually deliver more expensive traffic. So the question wasn't just whether installs are possible, but what the minimum install cost is at a normal traffic quality.

What was done

In the first stage I launched a series of test campaigns in Meta Ads optimized for Mobile App Installs. I tested different approaches:

  • Reels creatives;
  • Post creatives;
  • UGC format;
  • gameplay videos;
  • static ads;
  • different first-screen hooks;
  • separate campaigns for UK, US and Canada;
  • a combined Tier 1 campaign.

The logic was simple: not to guess one perfect creative, but to quickly gather data across several hypotheses and keep only the combos that deliver installs cheaper than the rest.

What the test showed

The key takeaway: the first funnel stage works. Users from Tier 1 countries respond to the game, go to the store and install the app. Install cost was held at $0.90–$0.92 on the best combos.

That's a good result for a test launch: Tier 1 markets are usually more expensive, and the game was not yet at full scale with large volumes of trained data.

Which hypotheses worked best

  • Reels creatives — the lowest CPI, $0.90: the short vertical format grabbed attention faster and fit mobile consumption better;
  • Simple gameplay demonstration — the mechanics were clear immediately, no need to figure out what's advertised;
  • The winning combo was moved to a dedicated campaign XENNA — T1 — WINNER, where CPI stayed at $0.92 at a larger install volume.
