Captured 60% of the Apple authorized service center market in 1 year on a $3,500 budget

60% of market in 12 mo $3,500 budget LTV 3+ years #1 in CIS

Took agency 4our into the niche of marketing for Apple authorized service centers: within 12 months 60% of the CIS market became clients — on a $3,500 marketing budget.

Project context

Advertising agency 4our was entering a new market — Apple authorized service centers (ASC). At the start the niche had almost no specialized contractors able to work within Apple's corporate requirements and the specifics of the ASC business.

I spotted the unmet need from insights gathered while working with industry players. To validate the hypothesis I ran interviews with owners and managers of service centers.

Objectives

  • Test demand for specialized marketing services for Apple ASCs.
  • Identify the key pains and constraints of the market.
  • Develop an offer aligned with Apple's requirements and the niche.
  • Build a high-ROI client-acquisition system.
  • Become the leading agency in the Apple ASC segment in the CIS.

What was done

Market research

Ran a series of customer-development interviews with managers of Apple authorized service centers.

Identified the key market problem: most agencies didn't understand Apple's internal requirements and couldn't work within the brand's corporate guidelines.

Studied Apple's requirements and marketing standards, defined the critical constraints and shaped a specialized offer for the niche.

After validating the hypothesis I tested the model on the first client and brought it to a stable result within 2–3 months.

Building the market-capture system

After a successful pilot I scaled the model through integrated marketing:

  • SEO
  • Performance Marketing
  • Content marketing
  • SMM
  • Partner marketing
  • Email marketing and work with the owned base

To acquire clients I built a funnel:

  • Targeted and paid search ads for ASC owners and managers.
  • Useful content on marketing and growth for service centers.
  • Building a subscriber base via lead magnets.
  • Expert positioning within the professional community.
  • Work with industry opinion leaders and referrals via closed professional chats.

The key growth driver was engaging an industry opinion leader who recommended the agency to the professional community.
