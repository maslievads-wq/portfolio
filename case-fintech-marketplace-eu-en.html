Launching and selling an EU fintech marketplace: from hypothesis to 20% ROI

20% ROI 3 EU markets Affiliate model Sold as an asset

Built and launched a financial-services marketplace for Germany, Poland and Spain: unified debit and credit cards, insurance and business banking into one catalog under an affiliate monetization model, validated unit economics at 20% ROI, and prepared the project for sale as a working digital asset.

Context and objective

The EU financial market is crowded with offers from banks, insurers and payment services, yet users struggle to quickly compare terms and pick the right product. The task was to launch a marketplace that unites offers on one platform, while validating the whole business model: traffic → product selection → handoff to partner → target action → affiliate payout.

Product hypothesis

The hypothesis: a unified catalog would simplify product selection and create a scalable affiliate model, monetized on an issued card, a confirmed application, an opened account, a purchased insurance product, or another target action defined by the partner.

MVP development

To validate the hypothesis with minimal spend, chose WordPress. It lowered development cost and time-to-market, allowed self-service updates to offers, fast rollout of new categories, and testing of different catalog structures — without investing in custom development before the model was proven.

Catalog structure

Banking products

  • Debit cards
  • Credit cards
  • Bank accounts
  • Business banking / cash management

Insurance products

  • Core insurance services
  • Retail consumer offers
  • Select partner insurance products

Financial services

  • Additional banking solutions
  • Products for private clients
  • Services for small business

Partner model

Onboarded affiliate networks, financial partner programs, CPA and CPM platforms, plus direct integrations with a number of financial services with no intermediaries.

  • Expanded the catalog and improved placement terms
  • Increased potential margin
  • Reduced dependency on a single affiliate network

Market selection

Chose Germany, Poland and Spain, evaluating each market separately on financial-services market size, digital-banking maturity, availability of partner programs, traffic cost, potential payout per target action, and competition level.

Go-to-market and performance testing

Google Ads

Captured users with already-formed demand on queries about choosing cards, comparing financial products, insurance, opening accounts, and financial services for business.

Meta Ads

Built demand through product offers, comparisons, category benefits, and localized creatives.

During testing, tracked CPC, CTR, cost per handoff to partner, conversion rate, revenue per user, channel payback, and ROI broken down by market, category, channel, partner program, and landing page. Underperforming combinations were cut and budget reallocated to profitable ones.

Model optimization

  • Reallocated traffic between categories
  • Prioritized the highest-earning products
  • Refreshed offers and landing pages
  • Tested new partners
  • Cut unprofitable combinations
  • Scaled the working sources

After validating the hypothesis and reaching stable results, assembled a full handover package: a working site, a product catalog, partner integrations, market-performance data, and validated unit economics. The project was sold as a working digital asset with a tested business model.
