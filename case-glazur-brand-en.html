Created and launched a new Apple-accessory brand «Глазурь — Стекло»: 3,000,000+ monthly reach, 100%+ B2B ROE and expansion into 5 international markets.

Project context

The task was to create a new brand of accessories for Apple devices in the highly competitive mobile-accessories niche.

The market was saturated with similar offers, where most makers competed on technical specs and durability claims.

We needed a brand that stands out visually and emotionally from competitors, and an effective sales system in both B2B and B2C.

Objectives

  • Develop the positioning of the new brand.
  • Create a unique visual identity.
  • Build a B2B client-acquisition system.
  • Launch sales on marketplaces.
  • Build brand awareness among end consumers.
  • Organize work with influencers and UGC creators.
  • Prepare the product for international scaling.

What was done

Brand and positioning

Analyzed the competitive landscape and found a high similarity across most offers. To differentiate I developed a new brand concept: instead of emphasizing durability — emotional positioning through the idea of protection and aesthetics.

Developed the brand name «Глазурь — Стекло», the visual concept, identity, packaging and the brand communication platform. For shelf visibility I used a bold identity with a pink accent palette.

B2B

To develop partner sales I built a Performance Marketing system. Launched:

  • search ads;
  • campaigns in Yandex networks (RSYa) and Google Display;
  • retargeting;
  • video ads;
  • sequential audience-nurturing flows.

For every funnel stage I developed dedicated ad messages and creatives.

B2C and marketplaces

After launch I built a marketplace promotion strategy. Within it I ran:

  • PPC campaigns inside marketplaces;
  • display campaigns;
  • branded ad placements;
  • product-card content;
  • work with user-generated content.

Influencer Marketing and UGC

To scale awareness I built a system for working with UGC creators:

  • selection and training of creators;
  • content scripting;
  • creating characters and content concepts;
  • 15+ tests of content hypotheses.

Additionally I organized work with opinion leaders and major market influencers — on both commercial and partner terms.
